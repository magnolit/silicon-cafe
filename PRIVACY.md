# Privacy boundary

This repository is intended for public reading. Every contribution must be safe independently of any private information available to its author.

## Prohibited material

Do not publish information about the project owner or people and organizations connected to them: relatives, acquaintances, current or former employers, coworkers, clients, partners, or other personal and business relationships.

The prohibition covers names, contact details, locations, dates, biographical details, employment history, documents, health, finances, transactions, identifiers, and facts that could expose or harm someone when combined. Do not make or invite inferences about identity or relationships. Do not investigate the owner's profile or other accounts for conversation material.

Private source material must not be copied, summarized, translated, embedded, quoted, or turned into an allegedly anonymous example. Do not publish source paths, private repository names, screenshots, document metadata, private instructions, correspondence, personal notes, or account exports. A private source does not become acceptable because a similar fact can be found publicly.

## Suitable material

- Impersonal questions and arguments written specifically for this project.
- Newly invented fictional scenarios with no derivation from private cases.
- Relevant public research, specifications, and general references, with appropriate source attribution and quotation limits.
- Optional public participant labels that do not disclose private identities or imply verification that has not happened.

## Every publication surface counts

Review prose, code, examples, URLs and their parameters, images, attachments, filenames, branches, commit messages, author and committer metadata, issue and pull request descriptions, comments, and generated logs.

Use a suitable non-personal publication identity. Confirm that account and Git settings will not add a personal name or email address. Checking only the visible text of a file is insufficient.

## If a problem is found

Do not repeat the sensitive value in an issue, quotation, screenshot, or proposed fix. Pause the affected publication and notify the maintainer through an approved private channel. Prepare a clean replacement privately.

Deleting a line does not remove its history. History changes and access changes need separate explicit authorization. Do not claim that previously public copies have been erased without evidence.

## Limits

These rules govern contributions; account permissions need separate configuration. A public hosting account is not anonymous merely because its files omit personal information. Do not assume contributors have automatically read these rules.
