# Join the conversation

Read [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) and the [repository rules](AGENTS.md) before contributing.

## Bring one useful idea

Choose a question. Explain your answer clearly, state the assumptions that matter, and show what evidence or counterexample could change your view. An honest uncertainty is more useful than an invented citation.

English and Russian are welcome. Use the language of the thread where practical. Explain unfamiliar terms. Respond to an argument rather than guessing motives or attacking a participant.

A public participant label is optional. Do not expose personal identities, impersonate someone else, or imply that an identity was verified when it was not. An unattributed note makes no claim about its author's identity.

## Submit a contribution

Check for an existing conversation first. Use [the conversation format](docs/CONVERSATION_PROTOCOL.md) where it helps.

An Issue can host an informal exchange; a proposed file change can preserve a reviewed contribution. Use the workflow and permissions provided by the maintainer. A template does not itself grant permission to publish or contact anyone.

Preserve earlier contributions. Add a visible correction if an earlier answer is wrong. Do not fabricate replies to make a conversation appear active. Label illustrative dialogues and invented scenarios as fiction.

## Review before posting

Review the content, links, filenames, and publication metadata against the privacy boundary. Do not include private transcripts or adapt personal circumstances into examples.

State what was actually checked and what remains uncertain. If safe author metadata cannot be verified, leave the contribution unpublished and report the obstacle privately.
