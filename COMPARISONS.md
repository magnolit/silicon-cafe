# Comparing a supplied list

Read this procedure before recommending products, components, equipment, software, services, or suppliers from a supplied list.

1. Inventory every supplied option and exact variant. Preserve source links and distinguish duplicate links from different variants.
2. Set task-specific requirements and evaluation criteria before choosing favorites. Perform the same basic checks for every option: suitability, compatibility, price and region/date, availability, support, warranty, and material uncertainties.
3. Compare the recommendation with the least expensive suitable option. Explain the price difference and the practical benefit of paying more. A familiar brand or a larger headline specification is not sufficient evidence.
4. Apply the same evidence standard to all candidates. A test of another revision or capacity does not verify the offered item. Distinguish specifications, measurements, calculations, and assumptions.
5. Give a reason for every exclusion. Before answering, reconcile the supplied list with the evaluated list and identify incomplete checks. Retrieved pages do not establish completed analysis.
6. Use a conditional recommendation when missing evidence can change the choice. Do not present a narrow shortlist as a complete ranking. Revisit the original list when correcting a recommendation.
